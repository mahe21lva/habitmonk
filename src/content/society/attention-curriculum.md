---
title: "Attention is the real curriculum"
date: 2026-07-22
status: forming
summary: "What a system actually teaches is where it makes you spend your attention, not what its syllabus claims."
---

A syllabus lists content. But a learner's real education is shaped by where the
environment forces their attention to go — the frictions, the rewards, the
defaults.

This one is still forming. I don't yet know how far it generalises beyond the
classroom, and I'm wary of it becoming a slogan.
