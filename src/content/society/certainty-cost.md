---
title: "I over-weighted certainty and under-weighted reversibility"
date: 2026-06-15
status: revised
summary: "The better question is rarely 'am I sure' but 'how cheaply can I undo this if I'm wrong'."
---

For years my decision instinct optimised for being *right*. I've revised toward
optimising for *reversibility* "how cheaply a choice can be unwound".

## The revision

The old view treated a confident wrong decision and a tentative wrong decision
as equally bad. They aren't, if the tentative one is easy to reverse. Certainty
is expensive to manufacture and often illusory; reversibility is a design
property you can actually build in.
