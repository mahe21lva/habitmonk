date: 2026-07-10
excerpt: "The habit of explaining the present through a tidy version of the past, and what it hides."

---

There's a comfortable way to read a country: pick the present, then select the
history that makes it inevitable. It's satisfying and it's a trap.

This essay is an attempt to catch myself doing it, to notice where I've
smoothed a contested history into a clean line pointing at today.
